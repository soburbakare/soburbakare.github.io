About

Who I am, plainly.

I'm an electrical engineer who works where the grid meets software. I have operated HV/MV distribution substations for over 3 years: vacuum and SF₆ circuit breakers, numerical protection relays, isolators, transformers, several other switch yard and control equipment and feeder monitoring.

Before that I serviced industrial power distribution equipment in the field, and I have built a background in embedded systems, hardware-software integration and industrial IoT.

My engineering work is informed by practical operational needs and an interest in rigorous, testable solutions for electricity distribution. That led to my applied engineering R&D project, Distribution Grid Data Acquisition & Edge Intelligence: an edge gateway that reads relay data over IEC 61850, decides locally what happened, and delivers it over MQTT, tested against IEC 61850 MMS Server simulator. It is the engineering foundation being developed into SpectaPower platform at DIMSYS Technologies.
